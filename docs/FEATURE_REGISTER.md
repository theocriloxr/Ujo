# Ujo OS Feature Register

## Product-wide foundations

- Offline-first edge hub; local LAN control; BLE/AP emergency onboarding fallback.
- Matter/Thread, Home Assistant, MQTT, Zigbee adapter strategy.
- Web/PWA, Android/iOS via Capacitor, desktop companion, watch/car interfaces later.
- Multi-home, multi-user, owner/co-admin/resident/child/guest/staff/technician roles.
- Trust & Control Center: sensors, retained data, learned facts, permissions, explanations, export/delete.
- Automation levels: manual, suggest-only, low-risk automatic, custom, emergency safety.
- Human-in-the-loop for high-risk actions.
- Manual physical overrides.
- Signed OTA, rollback, diagnostics, backups, and recovery.

## Ambient assistant

- Local wake word and user-defined assistant name.
- Distributed microphone/speaker satellites with physical mute.
- Phone as local/remote voice endpoint.
- Room-aware response routing, whisper/quiet hours, driving and sleep modes.
- Individual and household preferences with conflict resolution.
- Multilingual speech, intercom translation, accessibility modes.
- Home brief, pending approvals, reminders, and contextual suggestions.
- Conversation memory with scopes, expiration, correction, and “forget” controls.

## Energy and infrastructure

- Grid/inverter/generator/solar detection.
- Voltage/frequency/power-quality monitoring and safe load shedding.
- Appliance priority, compressor protections, restoration sequencing.
- Battery, solar, generator, diesel/fuel telemetry.
- Water tank, flow, leakage, pump health, dry-run and overflow protection.
- Internet/Wi-Fi health, failover, outage diagnosis.
- Savings, carbon/energy, runtime, and reliability reports.
- Neighborhood/estate alerts only through anonymized opt-in aggregation.

## Security and safety

- Locks/gates/garage, door/window, mmWave/presence, optional camera verification.
- Household roles, time-fenced staff, guest passes/QR, technician access.
- Duress workflow, incident timeline, tamper detection, privacy zones.
- Smoke/CO/gas/water leak integrations using certified devices.
- Occupancy simulation, away/night/vacation modes.
- Package/delivery and perimeter awareness where hardware supports it.
- Local-only camera/event processing by default with explicit recording controls.

## Food and household inventory

- Digital receipt parsing, barcode/manual entry, recurring purchases.
- Fridge/pantry/counter/freezer inventory.
- Shelf-life references, opened dates, temperature excursions, VOC trends, optional snapshots.
- Recipe/use-first suggestions, allergy/diet profiles, shopping list and geofenced reminders.
- Waste/savings reports and donation/compost suggestions.
- Household supplies, medication reminders (not medical diagnosis), filters and consumables.

## Maintenance and service

- Runtime, power signature, temperature, vibration, acoustic and environmental trends.
- Smart and “dumb” appliance profiles.
- Predicted service windows with confidence and evidence.
- Device/hub self-diagnostics and safe self-heal attempts.
- Installer/technician portal, work orders, parts, warranty, maintenance SLA, RMA.
- User-approved vendor dispatch and transparent quotes.

## Platform and business

- White-glove installation and commissioning.
- Hardware inventory, serial/lot traceability, secure manufacturing enrollment.
- Subscription entitlements that never disable core local control.
- Fleet health, staged OTA rings, support diagnostics with consent.
- Developer SDK, capability manifest, simulator, certification test suite, partner marketplace.
- Property developer/estate management and multi-property operations.
